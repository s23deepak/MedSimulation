What It Is

A clinical workbench, not a quiz screen.

Each session is built around the actions learners repeat in real clinical reasoning: focused history, exam selection, investigation ordering, prioritization, and post-case reflection.

Core Capabilities

Built around the clinical encounter.

H

AI Patient History

Interview AI patients whose answers reveal case-specific clinical clues.

E

Exam Findings

Request focused physical exam systems and compare findings against the case context.

L

Labs & Imaging

Order investigations, review results, and inspect ECG or imaging studies where available.

S

Structured Feedback

Submit a diagnosis and plan, then review domain scores and coaching points.

From case selection to debrief.

    Study domain scores, missed steps, and coaching points.

Educational Use

Designed for safe practice, not patient care.

MedSimulation is an educational simulation tool. Generated content and feedback should not be treated as medical advice, official clinical assessment, or a substitute for faculty review.
